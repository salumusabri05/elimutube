 1. What Is ElimuTube?
ElimuTube is a mobile phone application — like YouTube, but built specifically for Tanzanian teachers and students. It is a place where teachers can share their knowledge and earn money, and where students can access quality lessons in Swahili and English from the comfort of their phones.

Think of it this way: just as a shopkeeper can open an online shop on Jumia to sell goods to customers anywhere in Tanzania, a teacher can open a channel on ElimuTube to sell their lessons to students anywhere in Tanzania — without needing to be physically present in a classroom.

💡  The Simple Version
ElimuTube is YouTube + mobile money + Tanzania's school curriculum, designed specifically for unemployed and underemployed teachers to earn an income and for students to learn affordably.

 2. The Problem ElimuTube Solves
2.1  For Teachers
Tanzania trains thousands of teachers every year. Many of them are highly qualified — they hold degrees and diplomas in their subjects — but cannot find permanent teaching positions. They are either unemployed or working low-paying jobs unrelated to their training.

The knowledge and skill these teachers have built over years of study is sitting unused. There has been no easy way for a teacher in Mwanza, Mbeya, or Dodoma to share their expertise with students across the country and earn a fair income from it.

😔  The Reality Today
A qualified Mathematics teacher in Tabora may be unemployed or farming to survive, while students 500km away in Dar es Salaam are paying expensive private tutors for the same subject. ElimuTube connects them.

2.2  For Students
Students preparing for national examinations — especially Form IV (CSEE) and Form VI (ACSEE) — often struggle to access quality revision materials. Private tuition is expensive and only available in cities. School textbooks are frequently outdated or insufficient.

Existing global platforms like YouTube and Udemy do not solve this problem because:
•	Almost no content in Swahili for Tanzanian school subjects
•	You need a credit or debit card to pay — most Tanzanian students and families only have mobile money
•	Content is not organised around the Tanzanian national exam syllabus (NECTA)
•	Rural areas have slow internet — most platforms are not designed for low connectivity

 3. How ElimuTube Works
ElimuTube has two separate mobile phone apps — one for teachers and one for students. Both are available for download on Android and iPhone phones.

3.1  A Teacher's Journey on ElimuTube
Step	What the Teacher Does	What Happens
1	Downloads the app and registers	Creates a free account with their name, phone number, and subject area. Uploads a copy of their teaching certificate for verification.
2	Sets up their channel	Chooses a channel name (e.g. 'Mwalimu Juma's Mathematics'), writes a short description, and sets their monthly subscription price — for example, TSh 5,000 per month.
3	Records and uploads a lesson	Uses their phone to record a video lesson, then uploads it through the app. The platform automatically processes the video so it plays smoothly even on slow internet.
4	Adds notes and a quiz	Uploads a PDF of lesson notes and optionally taps one button to let the AI automatically create quiz questions from the lesson content.
5	Students subscribe and pay	Students see the channel, subscribe, and pay via M-Pesa, Airtel Money, or Tigo Pesa — all from their phones, with no bank account needed.
6	Receives earnings	On the 5th of every month, ElimuTube automatically sends the teacher's earnings (70% of subscription fees collected) directly to their M-Pesa number.

3.2  A Student's Journey on ElimuTube
Step	What the Student Does	What Happens
1	Downloads the app	Free to download. Creates an account with their name and phone number in under two minutes.
2	Searches for a subject	Types 'Form 4 Chemistry' or 'Biology CSEE' and sees a list of teacher channels. Can preview the first lesson of any channel for free before subscribing.
3	Subscribes to a teacher	Taps 'Subscribe', enters their phone number, and confirms a mobile money payment. Instant access — no waiting, no bank account needed.
4	Watches lessons	Videos play in Swahili or English (or both — the teacher can add subtitles). Can slow down or speed up the video. Can download for offline watching.
5	Takes quizzes and asks questions	After each lesson, a short quiz tests understanding. The student can also open an AI chat and ask questions like 'Can you explain photosynthesis again in simpler words?'
6	Joins a live class	When their subscribed teacher schedules a live class, the student gets a notification and can join from their phone — like a video call but with the whole class.

 4. Key Features Explained Simply
4.1  Video Lessons
Teachers record lessons on their phone or computer and upload them to ElimuTube. The platform automatically converts the video so it plays clearly even on a slow 2G or 3G connection — it adjusts the quality automatically based on how strong the student's internet is. Students can also download videos to their phone and watch them later with no internet at all.

4.2  PDF Notes
Alongside videos, teachers can upload written notes as PDF files — the same kind of notes they would photocopy and hand out in a physical classroom. Students can read these notes inside the app, highlight important parts, and refer back to them while revising.

4.3  Quizzes
Every lesson can have a quiz attached to it. Teachers can write the quiz questions themselves, or they can tap one button and the app's artificial intelligence will automatically read through the lesson notes and generate quiz questions. Students get immediate feedback after answering — they see which answers were correct and why.

4.4  Live Classes
A teacher can schedule a live online class — similar to a Zoom or Google Meet call, but built into the ElimuTube app. Subscribed students get a notification and can join from their phones. The teacher can share their screen (to show a diagram or working), and students can type questions in real time. Live classes are automatically recorded so students who missed them can watch later.

4.5  The AI Assistant
Every lesson has an AI chat assistant attached to it. Think of it as a very knowledgeable study buddy available 24 hours a day. A student can type a question like:

Student asks...	AI responds with...
"I don't understand what an enzyme is"	A simple, friendly explanation in Swahili or English, based on the specific lesson content
"Give me 5 more practice questions on this topic"	Five new questions generated on the spot, tailored to the lesson
"Summarise this lesson for me in bullet points"	A clear summary of the key points from the video and notes

The AI assistant only answers questions related to the lesson — it will not go off topic or give students irrelevant answers.

4.6  Offline Mode
Tanzania has many areas where internet connection is slow or unreliable, especially in rural regions. ElimuTube is designed with this in mind. Students can download any lesson — the video and the notes — to their phone while they have internet access (for example, at school or a WiFi hotspot), and then watch everything later without needing any connection at all.

4.7  Swahili and English
The entire app works in both Swahili and English. Teachers can upload lessons in either language or both. Video subtitles are automatically generated in both languages. Students choose their preferred language in the app settings. This makes ElimuTube accessible to students at all levels, including those who are more comfortable in Swahili than English.

 5. How the Money Works
5.1  How Students Pay
Students pay using the mobile money they already use every day — M-Pesa, Airtel Money, or Tigo Pesa. There is no need for a bank account or a credit card. The payment process works exactly like paying a bill via mobile money: the student enters their phone number, a payment prompt appears on their screen, they confirm with their PIN, and they are immediately given access to the teacher's lessons.

📱  No Bank Account Needed
ElimuTube was designed specifically so that any Tanzanian with a mobile phone and a mobile money account can pay for lessons. This is critical for reaching students in all regions of Tanzania, not just those in cities with bank accounts.

5.2  How Teachers Earn
Teachers set their own monthly subscription price when they create their channel. The recommended range is TSh 2,000 to TSh 20,000 per month, depending on the subject, level, and how much content they offer.

When a student subscribes and pays, the money is split automatically:

Who Gets It	How Much
The Teacher	70% of the subscription fee
ElimuTube Platform	30% (covers running costs)

On the 5th of every month, ElimuTube automatically calculates each teacher's earnings from the previous month and sends the full amount directly to their registered M-Pesa or Airtel Money number. No manual request needed.

5.3  Earnings Example
🧮  Example Calculation
Mwalimu Amina teaches Biology and sets her subscription at TSh 8,000 per month. By the end of her first year, she has 200 active subscribers. Her monthly gross income is TSh 1,600,000. After the 30% platform fee, she receives TSh 1,120,000 directly to her M-Pesa every month — without leaving her home.

This is not a maximum — teachers with popular channels covering high-demand subjects like Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry for Form 4 and Form 6 can build significantly larger subscriber bases over time.

 8. The Technology — Explained Simply
This section explains how ElimuTube's technology works using everyday comparisons. No technical knowledge is required to understand this.

Technical Term	What It Actually Means
Mobile App (Flutter)	ElimuTube is a phone application — like WhatsApp or Instagram. It works on both Android (Samsung, Tecno, Itel, etc.) and iPhone. It is downloaded once from the app store and lives on your phone.
Cloud Storage	All videos and documents are stored on powerful computers in secure data centres — not on your phone. This is why you can access them from any device and why they are safe even if your phone is lost or broken.
Video Streaming (HLS)	When you watch a lesson, the video is sent to your phone in small pieces, not all at once. This is how Netflix and YouTube work. It means the video starts playing quickly and adjusts its quality based on your internet speed.
Offline Download	You can save any lesson directly onto your phone's internal storage. Once saved, you can watch it anywhere — on a bus, in a village, in an area with no internet — just like a movie saved on a laptop.
Mobile Money Integration	ElimuTube is directly connected to M-Pesa, Airtel Money, and Tigo Pesa. When you pay, the money goes from your mobile wallet through a secure connection to ElimuTube, and then to the teacher — all automatically, in seconds.
Artificial Intelligence (AI)	The AI assistant in ElimuTube is a very advanced computer programme that can read and understand text and answer questions about it. It has been trained on enormous amounts of educational content. When a student asks it a question, it reads the lesson and generates a helpful answer — similar to having a knowledgeable tutor available at any time of day.
Live Classes (Agora)	The live class feature uses the same technology that powers large-scale video calls. It is stable enough for hundreds of students to join one teacher's live session at the same time, even on mobile internet connections.
Automatic Subtitles	When a teacher uploads a video, ElimuTube automatically creates subtitles — like the text you see at the bottom of a film. These are generated in both Swahili and English, making lessons accessible to all students.

 9. Safety, Trust and Quality
9.1  Teacher Verification
Before a teacher can publish any content and start earning, they go through a verification process managed by the ElimuTube team:
•	They upload a copy of their teaching certificate or degree
•	They provide their Tanzania national ID
•	The ElimuTube team reviews and approves the application within 48 hours
•	Only verified teachers receive a blue tick badge on their channel

This process ensures that students and parents can trust that the people teaching on ElimuTube are genuinely qualified.

9.2  Content Quality
•	Students can rate lessons and leave reviews after watching
•	If a lesson receives consistently poor ratings, the ElimuTube team reviews it
•	Students can report content that is incorrect, inappropriate, or misleading
•	The admin team can remove any content that does not meet quality standards

9.3  Student Privacy and Safety
•	Student data is stored securely and never sold to third parties
•	Students do not share personal information with teachers directly — all communication is through the platform
•	Payment information is processed securely — ElimuTube never stores mobile money PINs or passwords
•	The AI assistant is restricted to educational topics only
